# Living cricket career — first complete arc

Approved direction: user said “yes lets do it” to the five-stage implementation and asked to retain the established visual direction. Build one complete selection-pressure arc first, then expand the same event foundation. The career should feel like cricket rather than data entry.

First slice: identify a current regular with three genuinely low dismissed innings from saved scorecards; present a credible fit bench alternative of similar batting position; remember the XI actually chosen; report the actual performance and follow the pair for three fixtures. No mandatory dialogue, invented quotes, hidden morale penalty, or forced selection. Existing form, injury, conditions and role-fit mechanics remain authoritative.

Persist a bounded, versioned story journal in the existing career snapshot. Record only at a completed match action; never on rendering/replay. Use deterministic IDs and copy selection, migrate absent/invalid optional journals safely, isolate careers and seasons. Existing saves work without a journal. The first slice does not implement promises, transfer demands, relationship effects or the other proposed arc types.

Visual: compact matchday fixture with opaque charcoal and saturated club blocks, black frames, bounded ground image, white text, useful conditions and one dominant Watch match action. A short story connects matchday, XI review, opening playback and result. Avoid nested card stacks, pastel/highlighter colours and repetitive notices. Respect reduced motion and enlarged text.
