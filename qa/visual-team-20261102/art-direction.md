# GCC art direction audit

Scope: read-only source audit of build33 and actual archived screenshots. Viewed `qa/management-20261101/browser-build30-round3.png`, `browser-build31-promise.png`, `browser-build32-offseason.png`. These are builds30–32, **not build33 render evidence**. New capture directory was unavailable when this report was written.

## Priorities and root causes

1. **P0 — Two component art systems coexist. Evidence: source.** `qa/perfection-20261030/patch.py:176–177` injects arcade-charcoal.css then decision-ui.css. `.gccui` declares blue charcoal panels, rules and inset colors; legacy `:root` declares neutral gray panels. A scene transition can change material, border weight and spacing without changing game context. Consolidate shared tokens in one source; keep scene-specific CSS structural.
2. **P0 — Accent lacks semantic ownership. Evidence: archived build30.** Orange Watch match, yellow active navigation, yellow card tops and large yellow club fill all compete. Source `.g8button.g8primary`, `.g8careernav .active`, `.g8dressingroom`, and `.gccuiClubHead` assign accents independently. Reserve warm orange for the next meaningful action; club color lives in badge, identity strip and team comparisons; selected navigation uses a restrained mark and raised neutral surface.
3. **P1 — Hierarchy is expressed as repeated framing. Evidence: archived builds30/32.** KPI slab, match card, dressing room, player rows and controls carry comparable bright borders. Source arcade `.g8sheet`, `.g8dressingroom`, `.g8button`, plus decision `.gccuiPanel` use bright 1–2px boundaries and shadows. Use one strong outer panel boundary, dim row dividers, and inset cells only where values form a true group.
4. **P1 — Type has weight but insufficient role distinction. Evidence: archived builds30/31 and source.** A bold player, panel heading and command label are visually similar. Build33 resets `--display` to Arial while retaining 900 italic uppercase modal title styling; requested 850/900 weights on an Arial stack do not guarantee distinct real font faces. Adopt actual available weights 400/700, a distinct 28–32px page title, 20px section heading, 16px player name, 14–15px body, 12px metadata. Keep italic condensed treatment exclusive to a small branded display moment, if retained at all.
5. **P1 — Player identity is split into disconnected fragments. Evidence: archived build31.** Name sits alone in modal header; nationality/age/personality cluster near centre; rating floats at far right. Source `.g8modalhead`, `.g8playercontext`, `.g8analysisoverall`. Introduce one bounded player identity grid: name and role on left, bio underneath, rating attached on right; keep management tabs below that block. Validate against build33 before modifying markup.
6. **P1 — Alignment follows individual cards rather than a page scaffold. Evidence: archived build30.** Venue text starts at photo edge plus a separate gap, commands sit in their own right cluster, KPI cells use different insets, dressing room actions float at the right. Source `.g8decisionline`, `.g8dressingroom`, `.gccuiPanelHead`, `.gccuiTurn`. Establish 24px page gutters, 20px panel inset and a shared 16px rhythm; each row has explicit identity/content/action tracks, with names and descriptions sharing the same start.
7. **P2 — Club identity consumes attention instead of locating the user. Evidence: archived build30.** Crest, yellow GLO tile and repeated home/away club fills occupy different framing systems. Source `.g8brand`, `.g8badge`, `.gccuiClubHead`. Preserve approved crest and exact club identity; standardize their containers and create one persistent compact club signature. Large club fills belong in a deliberate match comparison, not generic panel trim.
8. **P2 — Texture, gradients, inset borders and shadows accumulate without a clear material model. Evidence: source; effect visible in archived builds.** `#g8app` combines radial and repeating gradients, header gradients, panel shadows and per-button shadows; `.gccuiPlayerScout` adds another gradient family. Choose flat charcoal surfaces and one subtle elevation treatment. Preserve supplied cricket icons at consistent 20/24px sizes and attach each to a meaningful label or bounded tile.

## Proposed art standard

Premium cricket operations desk: neutral graphite canvas `#17191a`, panel `#242729`, inset `#1d2022`, raised `#303538`; off-white `#f2f3f2` text, cool gray `#bbc2c6` secondary, dim `#41484d` rules. Orange `#ed7547` denotes the primary action, never every card edge. Exact club colors remain authored and ratings keep a legible semantic scale.

Use a consistent 4/8px rhythm, 24px page gutters, 20px panel padding, 16px component gaps, 48px controls, 6–8px corner radii. Every scene needs one dominant decision area and a quieter supporting column. Bind all text to a panel, row, identity block or deliberately aligned page heading. Tables use stable columns, tabular numerals and restrained dividers. Keep supplied imagery honest: venue photos belong in venue cards; crest remains crisp, unaltered and visually anchored. No ornamental glow or bright border on every object.

Acceptance requires new build33 screenshots at identical viewport and zoom across welcome, setup, draft, hub, selection, player modal, match and offseason. Source audit predicts inconsistency; it cannot establish actual build33 rendering defects or claim any fix passed.

## Build33 capture update — first implementation pass

Viewed actual `captures/home-desktop.png` and `captures/offseason-desktop.png`. These are now build33 rendering evidence. The following supersedes the orange-only career accent suggestion above: the user wants strong team themes. Preserve exact authored club colors. Orange is the preclub action/brand accent; once a club is selected, its exact `--club-accent` and `--club-ink` control the primary action, selected state and coherent career chrome. Do not alter club color values or suppress identity to achieve neutrality.

### Three highest-leverage fixes

1. **Unify material and career accent ownership.** Build33 offseason visibly combines orange HUD underline with yellow heading edge, yellow progress state and yellow summary edge. Those competing systems interrupt club ownership. Drive career HUD rule, primary control and selected state from the same club variables; keep neutral graphite surfaces/rules shared between legacy and `.gccui` components. Use club color on a small number of structural anchors, preserving a strong club-colored identity block. Reduce bright borders on ordinary rows and neutral cards so those team anchors become more effective.
2. **Give the home scene a real title composition.** Actual home capture has one crest in the global header and another centred inside an exceptionally large blank area of the welcome card. The only nearby title is the small uppercase date kicker; body prose is the main left-aligned text anchor. Preserve the approved crest, but bind the large crest to a deliberate title/wordmark block and give the scene an explicit game name and short, strong invitation. Align title, premise, stats and actions on the same content edge. Keep the existing supplied venue photo, framed as a purposeful companion to that game proposition. Avoid making the title merely another floating label.
3. **Resolve the offseason summary's unexplained dead area.** Actual offseason panel ends around x1086; three metrics and their top/bottom rules stop near x726, leaving its right third empty while descriptive text wraps earlier. The panel heading and top accent continue across the whole card. First pass: stretch the three equal metric tracks across the usable inner width and align the supporting copy and retirement row with that grid; alternatively use an intentional two-column composition if a real action/decision module belongs on the right. Keep every number attached to its label and give the panel one coherent inset. Verify below-fold actions before deciding the latter approach.

The actual captures support these composition/color findings. They do not support any claim about unseen build33 draft, selection, match or player-modal layout; those earlier comments remain archived evidence or source hypotheses.
