## What this establishes

The user’s concern is justified as an audit question: peripheral abilities and generated career templates include authored priors. Some template fielding, running, temperament and leadership values inherit a player’s peak anchor plus deterministic variation. They are not independently measured skills. A high core or overall rating cannot establish that a player deserves a high value in each of those areas.

The high-value list below is a review queue, not a finding that each player is wrongly rated. Pre-debut players are excluded from this 2008 queue. Full provenance and all profiles are in ratings-audit.json.
