# GCC player validation protocol

Automated and designer-led inspection cannot complete these human gates. No participants have been recruited or observed in this pass.

## Uncoached first session
Recruit 3–5 unfamiliar players with differing cricket knowledge. Supply the exact candidate hash and an empty browser profile. Ask them to take over a club and play their first match. Do not explain controls or selection strategy; record time, wrong turns, help requests and moments they would stop. Ask permission before recording.

Observe: league recruitment versus club draft distinction; player card comprehension; finding phase comparisons; choosing a keeper and bowling coverage; manual XI/order; finding analysis; pacing/skip; result interpretation. After the match ask what they would change and why. Success: no unrecoverable wrong turn, understandable primary actions and an accurate account of what recruitment/selection/results mean. Do not treat a small sample as statistically representative.

## Return session and career attachment
Ask participants to return voluntarily, then play through an annual transition. Observe retention, releases, departure allowance, trade cutoff, retirement replacement and draft rights. At later-career checkpoints ask which players matter to them, what changes next season, and whether they would continue. Showing someone a prefilled year15 save is not evidence of nineteen-season engagement.

## Device and accessibility sessions
Use actual iPhone/Safari and Android hardware, desktop Firefox, keyboard-only navigation and at least one regular screen-reader user. Test text enlargement, touch selection/reordering, background/resume, storage denial/full storage, offline images and interrupted matches. Emulated viewports/engines supplement this; they do not replace it.

## Issue handling
Record observation → hypothesis → minimal change → repeat task with a new participant if learning would bias the result. Prioritise inability to proceed, lost progress, misunderstood irreversible decisions, then repeated friction. Never declare enjoyment or comprehension passed because buttons exist.
