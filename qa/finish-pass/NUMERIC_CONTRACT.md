# Numerical contract and release evidence

Player ability: 1–99 ordinal model estimates. Overall is a rounded role-based composite of underlying skills for newly generated careers using overallMethod=skill-overall-v1. All-rounders receive at least the rating of identical batting or bowling specialists: the maximum of their AR, BAT and BOWL role composites. This describes strongest specialist-equivalent ability, not complete utility or historical certification. Form is excluded from overall; execution/form and fitness remain explicit contexts. Existing saved authored overalls and realised futures retain their original method. The preceding private role-fit-v1 evaluator also remains frozen. The global roleFit function is unchanged because legacy saved futures depend on it.

Phase scores: deterministic attribute composites (exact formulas embedded in the playable). They are estimates, not historical strike rates or win probabilities. Team phase profiles include opportunity, role/depth coverage, keeping, fielding, continuity and fitness context. XI overall is an aggregate profile estimate, not a mean of roster overall or predicted win percentage.

Private career variation: bounded, seeded saved families/timing. It is designed alternate-history uncertainty, not historical evidence. No annual reroll. Existing saved trajectories must remain identical through restore.

Evidence: downloaded Cricsheet ledgers and player register have exact URLs/hashes. Identity source links do not establish skill confidence. Contemporary three-year windows use competition/year/phase contextual rates, batting prior180 balls and bowling prior240 balls, retaining sample size and shrinkage weight. These priors and comparison weights are audit assumptions, not scientifically fitted constants.

Assessment limitations: historical six-over powerplay differs from GCC four-over opening + two-over surge; competition strength and opposition need further adjustment; register initials/surname matches need identity review; sparse phase records cannot certify elite skill. Fielding and keeping opportunities, dropped catches, leadership, temperament, variations and technique are not comprehensively measured by these archives. Never derive precise ratings for these from missing evidence or a biography URL. A rating outlier is a review lead, not an automatic historical correction.

Release requirements: every shipped player has reviewed identity and an attribute-level measured/proxy/estimated provenance ledger; a reproducible rating-generation method with bounded sparse-sample treatment; held-out period/competition validation; simulation effects and scoring distributions assessed separately; all supported saves migrate without changing established outcomes. This pass builds the source assessment and fixes numerical inconsistencies; it cannot certify the remaining authored attributes as perfected.
