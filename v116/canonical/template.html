FOUNDATION YEAR · 2008

The breakaway begins.

It is July 2008. Saudi money has landed in cricket, and eight established clubs have agreed to break away for a new global T20 competition: Global Champions Cricket.

The clubs keep their names and home grounds. Their playing lists do not come with them. Every dressing room starts empty, and every player must decide whether to enter the new league.

You arrive before the first player has signed. Five days of calls decide who enters the pool. Then comes the draft: 120 picks, eight new squads, 14 matches and the first GCC title.
